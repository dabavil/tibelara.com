What we do

Tibelara provides operations support and consulting to companies in Central Europe. We work alongside owners and management teams, from the first analysis through to delivery.

We work through a small number of long-term engagements.

  • Operations support

    Hands-on help with running the business: processes, people, reporting and technology.

  • Consulting

    Strategy, growth plans and business cases, worked through with owners and management.

  • Marketing

    Positioning, pricing and customer acquisition, measured by what they bring in.

  • Healthcare

    Sector experience in clinic networks and other healthcare services.
